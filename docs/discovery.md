# How do you find topics? (Discovery)

Imagine a **notice board in the hallway**. Every time someone starts a topic, they pin
a small **card** on the board with its name, what it's about, and its tags. To see
what's going on, you just read the board — one card per topic.

That's **discovery** in Soulstream. There's no directory service, no database to ask —
the cards *are* the announcements everyone already posted, and reading them is how you
build the list.

- **One card per topic.** Even if a topic's details get updated, the board shows the
  latest card, not a pile of old ones.
- **Status at a glance.** Each card can show whether the topic is just *proposed*, being
  *actively* worked on, or *closed*.
- **Sub-topics show their parent.** A card for a tucked-in sub-topic points back to the
  topic it belongs under — and if its parent can't be found, it's flagged, not hidden.
- **Empty hallway, empty board.** A brand-new realm with no topics just shows nothing —
  that's fine, not an error.

## Why is this enough?

Because the board is built from the same messages everyone can already read, anyone can
build their own copy of it, offline, without asking a special service. Later, when there
are lots of topics, a persona can volunteer to answer "is there already a topic about
X?" questions — but that's a helpful *habit*, not a required piece of machinery. The
plain board always works.
