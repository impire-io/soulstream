# SoulNode Vision

## What SoulNode is

SoulNode is the **single-binary distribution of the Soulstream ecosystem**:
one `soulnode` binary that puts the whole stack on a machine you own — the
NATS server (operator mode, JetStream) embedded, [SoulIdentity](../../../soulidentity)
as the identity plane, the archivist as memory, [soulrealm](../../../soulrealm)
as the runtime, and an MCP front door a Claude client can reach.
`soulnode init` performs the first-boot ceremony; `soulnode up` brings the
realm to life and prints the URL to point a client at.

Soulstream is the record, soulrealm is the room, SoulIdentity is the name;
SoulNode is the **house** — the place where all of them stand together on
hardware you control.

## The founding bet

**Owning your realm should cost one binary and one command.** The ecosystem's
admission model, identity plane, and record shape are not simplified to fit on
a laptop; the laptop runs the same shape a hosted deployment runs —
operator-mode NATS, auth-callout admission, a sealed vault, attributable
personas. SoulNode bets that the full shape, correctly composed, fits in one
process — and that nothing less is worth self-hosting, because a simplified
local shape would prove nothing about the real one.

## Who it is for

Independent professionals — people who want their realm (their record, their
keys, their agents) on hardware they control, without an operations team, a
Kubernetes cluster, or a NATS credentials tutorial. Download one binary, run
two commands, point a Claude client at the printed URL, and start working.

## Where it is pointed

Horizon ambitions, not schedulable milestones — each shapes design decisions
today and sits behind a named research gate (tracked in
[`../03-IMPLEMENTATION/roadmap.md`](../03-IMPLEMENTATION/roadmap.md)):

- **The bundle.** Every plane of the ecosystem in one process — server,
  identity, memory, runtime, front door — each consumed through its public,
  tagged surface. Workloads stay outside the process (soulrealm's backends);
  everything else is in it.
- **First boot as the product.** The whole ceremony — operator, accounts,
  signing keys, sentinel, sealed vault, buckets — generated by `soulnode
  init` with zero manual key steps. The distance from download to a working
  realm is the metric that matters.
- **The front door on your own network.** MCP over HTTPS on a tailnet: the
  host's `tailscale serve` first, an embedded tailnet node (tsnet) when it
  measurably earns its weight. A no-install client (claude.ai, Claude
  Desktop) connects with a static bearer and nothing else.
- **Day 2 without an operator.** Upgrade, backup, restore — the state
  directory *is* the realm, and moving it to a new machine is a copy, not a
  migration project.

## What we refuse to become

- **A fourth home for domain logic.** SoulNode wires; it does not invent.
  Identity behavior belongs to SoulIdentity, runtime behavior to soulrealm,
  record behavior to soulstream. If a feature needs new domain logic, that
  logic lands upstream first (constitution I). This is non-negotiable.
- **A dev-mode fork.** No simplified auth lane, no "local only" bypass, no
  admission shortcut the hosted ecosystem doesn't have. What SoulNode proves
  locally must hold hosted, and vice versa (constitution II).
- **A hosting platform.** SoulNode runs *your* realm on *your* machine. It is
  not a control plane, not a multi-tenant service, and not an enterprise
  deployment tool.
- **The architecture.** Every component remains independently deployable
  without SoulNode; SoulNode is a distribution of the ecosystem, never its
  definition. The day a component only works inside SoulNode, something has
  gone wrong.

## How ambition stays honest

Every ambition above sits behind a named, pre-registered gate, and no build
outruns its gate. Direction decisions record what would change our minds when
they are made, so a future reversal is a clean, anticipated turn instead of
drift. The full discipline lives in [`constitution.md`](constitution.md) and
[`how-we-work.md`](how-we-work.md).
